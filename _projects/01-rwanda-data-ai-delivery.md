> **Ongoing engagement · 2026–present**  
> Details are deliberately generalised to respect confidentiality obligations.

## The delivery context

At S4D Consulting, I lead technical delivery across data engineering,
generative AI, and deployment operations for analytical applications supporting a
pharmaceutical-intelligence initiative in Rwanda.

The work sits where data, AI capability, and dependable technical delivery meet.
It is not treated as a one-off build: each workstream needs to support the wider
application and the people who will work with it.

## My role

I lead the data-engineering, generative-AI, and DevOps workstreams, coordinate a
small engineering team, and collaborate with institutional stakeholders. My role
connects the technical direction of the work with the practical delivery needed
for analytical applications.

## Current focus

### Data engineering

Leading data-engineering work that supports analytical applications and the
reliable use of their underlying information.

### Generative AI

Leading the generative-AI workstream, with attention to use cases that are
appropriate for the analytical context rather than treating AI as a standalone
feature.

### Deployment operations

Leading the deployment-operations workstream so that technical delivery remains
connected to how applications are introduced, supported, and maintained.

## How the work comes together

The three workstreams are closely connected: useful analytical applications
depend on dependable data; generative-AI components need to fit a clear use case;
and both need practical operational foundations. Treating those concerns together
helps keep technical choices aligned with the delivery context.

## Status

The initiative is in progress. This case study describes the scope of my role and
current engineering focus only. It does not disclose client data, internal
systems, implementation detail, or outcome claims.
